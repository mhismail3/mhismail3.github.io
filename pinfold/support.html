Pinfold — Support

Updated 7 November 2026

Pinfold is an original arcade puzzle game. Swipe across the page to fold walls, pen the moving strays, and reach each level's fill target. A stray hitting an unfinished wall costs a life.

Purchases
Pinfold has six optional in-app purchases (US prices): Remove Ads $2.99 and the Starter Bundle $4.99 (Remove Ads plus 300 gems), both one-time; and gem packs Handful $0.99, Pouch $4.99, Chest $9.99 and Vault $19.99.

Remove Ads
The one-time Remove Ads purchase, also included in the Starter Bundle, removes between-level interstitial ads. It does not unlock levels or change gameplay. To recover a purchase, use Settings → Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to buy it. Pending purchases remove ads only after Apple approves them. If a purchase is refunded or revoked, the entitlement ends. Apple manages billing and refunds at https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ .

Gems and Customize
Gems are cosmetic only: they unlock styles for balls, fill, backgrounds and pen lines in Customize, and never unlock levels or change gameplay. You earn gems by playing or buy them in the Gem Shop. Purchased gem packs are consumable and are added once per purchase. The Starter Bundle's gems return after Restore Purchases. If iCloud is on for Pinfold, your gem balance and unlocked styles sync between your devices through Apple's iCloud key-value store under your own Apple Account. The developer cannot see this data.

Progress and offline play
Level progress is stored on this device. There is no login or cloud-save account. Reinstalling the app can lose game progress. Offline play remains available; ads that cannot be loaded are simply skipped. A network connection may be needed for a purchase or restore.

Privacy
Read the Privacy Policy at https://mhismail3.github.io/pinfold/privacy.html . Privacy Choices appears in Settings when the advertising consent SDK requires it. Ads are non-personalized, any required consent is asked before ads load, and Pinfold never shows a tracking prompt or tracks you. Buying Remove Ads or the Starter Bundle stops future ad requests but does not delete data previously held by Apple or Google.

Report an issue
Open an issue at https://github.com/mhismail3/mhismail3.github.io/issues . Include app version, iOS version, device model and steps to reproduce. This is a public support channel: do not post Apple Account passwords, receipts, payment data, identity documents or other personal information. Apple handles billing and refund requests; Google handles information processed by its advertising services.
