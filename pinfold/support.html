Pinfold — Support

Updated 21 October 2026

Pinfold is an original arcade puzzle game. Swipe across the page to fold walls, pen the moving strays, and reach each level's fill target. A stray hitting an unfinished wall costs a life.

Remove Ads
The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes between-level interstitial ads. It does not unlock levels or change gameplay. To recover a purchase, use Settings → Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to buy it. Pending purchases remove ads only after Apple approves them. If a purchase is refunded or revoked, the entitlement ends. Apple manages billing and refunds at https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ .

Progress and offline play
Progress is stored on this device. There is no cloud-save account. Reinstalling the app can lose game progress. Offline play remains available; ads that cannot be loaded are simply skipped. A network connection may be needed for a purchase or restore.

Privacy
Read the Privacy Policy at https://mhismail3.github.io/pinfold/privacy.html . Privacy Choices appears in Settings when the advertising consent SDK requires it. Buying Remove Ads stops future ad requests but does not delete data previously held by Apple or Google.

Report an issue
Open an issue at https://github.com/mhismail3/mhismail3.github.io/issues . Include app version, iOS version, device model and steps to reproduce. This is a public support channel: do not post Apple Account passwords, receipts, payment data, identity documents or other personal information. Apple handles billing and refund requests; Google handles information processed by its advertising services.
