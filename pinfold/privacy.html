Pinfold — Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Pinfold is developed by Mohsin H Ismail. This policy describes the ad-supported iOS app and its optional one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Game data
Progress, scores, sound and haptics settings are stored locally on your device. Pinfold has no account, chat, location permission, or developer-operated gameplay server. Removing the app may remove local progress.

Purchases
Apple processes the one-time $2.99 Remove Ads purchase. Pinfold checks verified StoreKit transactions to determine whether ads should be removed, including after a restore or refund. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Restore Purchases uses your Apple Account and may ask you to sign in. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Non-personalized advertising
Pinfold does not request tracking permission. The app has no ATT authorization call, IDFA access code, or advertising-identifier explainer. Every Google AdMob request asks for non-personalized ads (NPA), disables publisher privacy personalization (PPT) and publisher first-party ID, and sets Google's limited-ads (LTD) and restricted-data-processing (RDP) signals. Optional Google SDK crash reporting and unused mediation initialization are disabled. Pinfold has no developer-operated advertising profiles, gameplay analytics server, or crash-reporting service.

These are ad-treatment requests and SDK settings, not a Google-certified guarantee of no collection, no identity linkage, or IDFA suppression. Removing a permission prompt does not revoke a grant previously made to an older version. Google controls its SDK and ad-serving behavior. If you previously allowed tracking in an older Pinfold test build, delete that installation and reinstall the current version to clear the old installation's permission state; this also removes local game progress.

Ads still involve data collection. Google may process your IP address (including IP-derived general location), device/app information, ad impressions and interactions, other product interactions, and diagnostic/performance information for ad serving, fraud prevention, service operation and measurement. Limited ads disable personalization and features requiring local identifiers; Google's programmatic limited ads can still use fraud-prevention-only local storage. These requests involve Google and its advertising services. Non-personalized does not mean “no data collected.” Google's general SDK disclosure also lists app/developer-scoped device identifiers; Pinfold disables publisher first-party ID and requests limited serving rather than using IDFA. See Google's SDK disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure and limited-ads explanation: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/10105530 . Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

Consent and choices
Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether regional consent is required, including in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Pinfold presents required consent before loading ads; the app never follows consent with an ATT authorization call. Any remotely configured Google IDFA message must also be disabled by the developer. Privacy Choices in Settings is available when required by the consent SDK. Use it to review or withdraw consent. An affirmative consent choice never enables personalized ads. If privacy setup fails or ad requests are not allowed, gameplay remains available without ads. Ads appear only at occasional completed-level breaks, never during play. Buying or restoring Remove Ads stops future ad loading and display; it does not erase information previously held by Google or Apple.

Retention and requests
Local settings remain until removed from the device. Google and Apple determine retention for information they process under their own policies. Contact those services for requests relating to their records.

Children
Pinfold does not include chat or user-generated content. If you have a question about a child's privacy, use the support page without posting personal information. Apple and Google also provide privacy controls for their services.

Contact and changes
Support: https://mhismail3.github.io/pinfold/support.html . You can report app issues or general privacy questions at https://github.com/mhismail3/mhismail3.github.io/issues . This is a public channel: do not post passwords, receipts, payment card details or other personal information. For information processed by Apple or Google, use those services' privacy-request channels. Changes to this policy will be reflected in its effective date.
