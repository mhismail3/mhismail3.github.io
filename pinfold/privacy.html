Pinfold — Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Pinfold is developed by Mohsin H Ismail. This policy describes the ad-supported iOS app and its optional one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Game data
Progress, scores, sound and haptics settings are stored locally on your device. Pinfold has no account, chat, location permission, or developer-operated gameplay server. Removing the app may remove local progress.

Purchases
Apple processes the one-time Remove Ads purchase. Pinfold checks verified StoreKit transactions to determine whether ads should be removed, including after a restore or refund. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Restore Purchases uses your Apple Account and may ask you to sign in. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Advertising and consent
The free version uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Ads are requested only when the consent SDK allows requests, and are shown only at occasional completed-level breaks. Google may process IP address (which may infer general location), app/developer-scoped device identifiers, ad interactions, product interactions, diagnostics and performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain its services. These requests involve Google's servers and partners; gameplay being local does not mean no data leaves your device.

This version requests non-personalized ads and does not request Apple's tracking authorization or access to the advertising identifier (IDFA). Non-personalized ads can still use identifiers and require consent or other privacy choices. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads .

Privacy Choices in Settings is shown when required by the consent SDK. Use it to review or change advertising consent. If privacy setup fails and ad requests are not allowed, gameplay remains available without ads. Buying Remove Ads stops future ad loading and display; it does not automatically erase information previously held by Google or Apple.

Retention and requests
Local settings remain until removed from the device. Google and Apple determine retention for information they process under their own policies. Contact those services for requests relating to their records. No developer analytics or crash-reporting service is added by Pinfold itself; Google SDK diagnostics are separate.

Children
Pinfold does not include chat or user-generated content. If you have a question about a child's privacy, use the support page without posting personal information. Apple and Google also provide privacy controls for their services.

Contact and changes
Support: https://mhismail3.github.io/pinfold/support.html . You can report app issues or general privacy questions at https://github.com/mhismail3/mhismail3.github.io/issues . This is a public channel: do not post passwords, receipts, payment card details or other personal information. For information processed by Apple or Google, use those services' privacy-request channels. Changes to this policy will be reflected in its effective date.
